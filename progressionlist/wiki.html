1. explanation

This document was created based on namuwiki 's MÚSECA/difficulty notation problem document, which is based on the format of BEMANIWiki 's morale/history song list list.

We recommend that you refer to the song's documentation for a detailed explanation of the transcription, but please note that there are some songs for which documentation has not yet been created.

2. Criterion

2.1. outline

  • Mark only cases that are at a level that many users can sympathize with, not personal impressions .
  • The level and constant notation will be based on the constants and notation difficulty of the international version and maimai DX CiRCLE currently in operation in Korea , but songs that are upgraded/downgraded in the version currently in operation in the Japanese domestic version will be marked with separate comments.
  • Starting with BUDDiES PLUS, the .6 also has a + sign.

2.2. Bull level/mul level/individual difference/caution

  • Bullep song (bulleb) satisfies the following criteria and is written in red .
    • If the song is more difficult than the average level song corresponding to the notation level.
    • The difficulty of the song is 1 level higher than the song or a constant of 0.1.[14↑] , 0.2[14↓] If it is similar to at least one of the higher songs
  • Mulrep (Mulreb) satisfies the following criteria and is written in blue .
    • If the song is of lower difficulty than the average level of the song corresponding to the notation level.
    • The difficulty of the song is 1 level higher than the song or a constant of 0.1.[14↑] , 0.2[14↓] If it is similar to at least one of the lower songs.
  • Individual difference songs satisfy the criteria below and are written in purple .
    • When it is recognized that the difficulty of the song varies greatly depending on the player.
    • When a particular pattern is concentrated in the transcription of the song .
  • The pattern note song satisfies the following criteria and is written in green .
    • The difficulty of the song itself does not deviate significantly from the notation level, but other conditions (slide placement prone to indirection, excessively fast/slow slide patterns, extreme after-sales, etc.) make it difficult to clear or score without prior knowledge .
    • If the difficulty of the song drops significantly when the above elements are known.
  • The scoring notes satisfy the following criteria and are written in orange (darkorange) .
    • When the scoring difficulty is significantly higher than the clear difficulty[5]
  • When more than 2 of the above 5 conditions are satisfied, the song title is expressed in the corresponding colors.


In principle, each item should be written in the following order: low level song → high level song → individual difference song → caution song → scoring caution song, and the same is true for composite score. If the songs are at the same level and have the same attributes, write them down first.

In principle, the difficulty level ( BASIC , ADVANCED , EXPERT , MASTER , Re:MASTER ) corresponding to the song is indicated after the song name , followed by the BPM.

If the song has both deluxe and standard scores, please indicate whether the score you wish to write is deluxe (DX) or standard (STD) to avoid confusion.

In the case of Re:MASTER transcription, please describe the version in which Re:MASTER transcription first appeared .
  • There are also songs that do not currently have documentation. This can be confirmed by the red underline when you hover your mouse over it.
